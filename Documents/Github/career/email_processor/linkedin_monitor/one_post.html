Hiring AI Engineer

Company: Lex and Link
Location: Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi (on-site preferred; hybrid negotiable)

Type: Full-time

Compensation: PKR 350,000–450,000/month, based on experience

About the Role
We're building an AI-powered engineering report pipeline for the US property insurance market—a system that takes property data, weather records, and roof imagery to produce citation-grounded, audit-ready observation reports for licensed Professional Engineers to review and seal.

This is not a "wrap an LLM around a prompt" project. The architecture deliberately limits the LLM to drafting, with determinations made by deterministic, unit-tested rules engines, citations resolved through retrieval against licensed references, and full provenance on every field. Defensibility is the product. If you've been burned by hallucinating chatbots in production and want to build something rigorous instead, this is that job.

You will be the first engineering hire, owning the technical build end to end while growing a small team as the project scales.

What You'll Build
A structured Fact Object pipeline: intake, geocoding, parcel/permit lookups, roof geometry ingestion, weather corroboration across APIs

A deterministic rules engine encoding published hail/wind damage thresholds (HAAG, ASTM, IBHS) with honest meets/below outputs

A RAG-constrained drafting engine: LLM prose generation with validators rejecting any number or citation not traceable to a verified source

A review console for licensed engineers: evidence linkage, edit logging, immutable audit trail, digital seal workflow
Integrations with third-party APIs: roof measurement (EagleView/HOVER), weather (NOAA, HailTrace), parcel data (Regrid/ATTOM), estimating (Xactimate)
Later: a computer vision model for roof damage detection and functional-vs-cosmetic classification trained on expert-adjudicated imagery

What We're Looking For
Must have:
5+ years of software engineering experience, including 2+ years shipping ML/AI systems to production
Strong backend skills (Python preferred)—design schemas, build services, write tests, and reason about data provenance
Hands-on experience with LLM applications: RAG pipelines, embeddings, retrieval quality, prompt and validation layers
Experience integrating third-party APIs behind clean interfaces
Judgment to know when not to use ML—comfortable building deterministic, auditable logic where correctness matters
Strong written English; you'll read US building codes and engineering standards

Nice to have:
Computer vision experience (object detection, image classification, model calibration)
Experience in insurance-tech, construction-tech, legal-tech, or another regulated/high-stakes domain
Familiarity with document processing, PDF generation, or audit/compliance tooling
Prior experience as a first or early engineering hire

To apply: Send your CV and written answer to careers@lexandlink.com with the subject line "AI Engineer".
